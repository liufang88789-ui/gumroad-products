Everything you need to launch your first Printful product.

## What's inside

### 1. Printful Setup Checklist
Step-by-step guide to setting up your Printful account, connecting stores, and understanding the POD fulfillment flow.

### 2. POD Product Category Guide
Best-selling POD product categories and why most beginners pick the wrong products first.

### 3. 20 POD Design Prompts
Curated prompts for generating POD-ready product designs and mockups.

### 4. Listing Optimization One-Pager
Quick tips for writing POD listings that actually convert on Etsy, Shopify, and similar platforms.

## Quick start

1. Set up Printful account
2. Connect your store (Etsy, Shopify, etc.)
3. Pick a product category from the guide
4. Use prompts to generate design ideas
5. List and optimize
