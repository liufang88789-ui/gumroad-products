# Printful Setup Checklist

## Step 1: Account Setup
- [ ] Go to printful.com and create a free account
- [ ] Choose your primary sales platform (Etsy, Shopify, etc.)
- [ ] Connect your store via Printful's integrations
- [ ] Verify billing and payment information

## Step 2: Product Selection
- [ ] Pick a product type to start with (recommend: stickers or t-shirts)
- [ ] Research competitor listings on your sales platform
- [ ] Identify a gap or unique angle for your design
- [ ] Start with 3-5 initial products, not 50

## Step 3: Design Sourcing
- [ ] Generate designs using provided prompts
- [ ] Use Canva, Photoshop, or similar for composition
- [ ] Export at 300 DPI minimum for apparel
- [ ] Export at high res for stickers (Printful specs per product)

## Step 4: Listing Creation
- [ ] Write title with main keywords (under 140 chars for Etsy)
- [ ] Write 3-5 bullet points highlighting features and benefits
- [ ] Add tags (Etsy: up to 13; use all of them)
- [ ] Set competitive but profitable pricing
- [ ] Upload product photos (mockup + flat lay)

## Step 5: Launch and Iterate
- [ ] Publish listing
- [ ] Drive initial traffic (social media, tags, shares)
- [ ] Monitor Printful dashboard for first orders
- [ ] Analyze what's working and double down

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Listing too many products before validating one
- Using low-resolution artwork
- Ignoring competitor pricing
- Overpricing for the niche
- Not connecting analytics to track traffic sources
