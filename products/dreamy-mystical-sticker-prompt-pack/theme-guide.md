# Theme Guide & Style Tips

## 6 Theme Categories

### 1. Moon & Stars (5 prompts)
Best for: Night-sky themed sticker shops, celestial aesthetic lovers
Style tips: Add "pastel purple", "soft glow", "silver tones" for dreamy vibe

### 2. Forest & Nature (4 prompts)  
Best for: Nature lovers, woodland aesthetic, organic themes
Style tips: Try "watercolor", "soft grain", "botanical illustration"

### 3. Crystal & Aurora (3 prompts)
Best for: Spiritual aesthetic, witchy/vintage vibes, iridescent lovers
Style tips: Add "iridescent", "prism", "glow effect", "holographic"

### 4. Cosmos & Galaxy (3 prompts)
Best for: Space enthusiasts, bold colors, deep contrast
Style tips: Add "nebula", "star cluster", "deep blue", "cosmic dust"

### 5. Floral & Botanical (3 prompts)
Best for: Soft feminine aesthetic, garden lovers, gift stickers
Style tips: Try "watercolor botanical", "pressed flower style", "elegant line art"

### 6. Spirit & Animal (2 prompts)
Best for: Whimsical shops, woodland creature fans, fantasy themes
Style tips: Add "whimsical", "fantasy illustration", "soft atmospheric"

## Quick Sticker Optimization Tips

1. **White background**: Most POD printers need transparent/white backgrounds
2. **300 DPI minimum**: Ensure your final images are 300 DPI for print quality
3. **Square aspect ratio**: Most sticker designs work best in 1:1 or 3:2 ratio
4. **Isolated elements**: Remove backgrounds in Canva or remove.bg before uploading to POD

## Extending the Collection

Use these prompts as a starting collection, then create variations:
- Change "purple" to "pink" or "blue"
- Swap "forest" for "ocean"  
- Add "- mystical" or "- celestial" to any nature prompt
- Try different AI models for different art styles
